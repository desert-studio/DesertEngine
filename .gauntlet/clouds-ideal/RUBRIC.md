# Lenses -- one per round, rotating

Format matters: `- **name** -- question`. `next` parses these.

- **silhouette** -- does the edge read as a surface, or as a smooth blob?
- **light** -- is there a gradient inside the body, and is shadow the right hue?
- **scale** -- does the subject read at the size and distance it is supposed to be?
- **coherence** -- does the whole dome agree with itself, or only the angle we shot?
- **grain** -- at h and m, is the surface free of sub-head grain, sparkle and loose flecks the bar never shows?
