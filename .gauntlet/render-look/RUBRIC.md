# Lenses -- one per round, rotating

Format matters: `- **name** -- question`. `next` parses these.

- **depth** -- does the grass carpet have depth (dark, occluded base; lit tips), or does it read as flat strips on a bright floor?
- **light** -- does sunlight read as a light (warm key, believable sky fill, shadow hue), and does the tonal range look filmed rather than rendered?
- **distance** -- do far planes recede (aerial perspective, contrast falloff) the way a photographed landscape does?
- **material** -- do grass, wood and dirt respond to light as their own materials (sheen, translucency, roughness), or as one plastic?
- **coherence** -- do wide, mid, low and barn views agree, or does the change flatter one angle?
