# Round 01 verdict - lens: depth
(Blindness is nominal: the subjects differ, BAR.md. Judged on the lens's question only.)

Prediction: the image whose grass carpet reads as a continuous mass wins on depth.
- One image's field is a continuous mass: the base of the stalks goes dark into the canopy, light lives in the
  upper third, and nothing of the floor shows through except in the far rows.
- The other's carpet reads as separate strips standing on a BRIGHT, sunlit, textured floor: the floor between
  blades is as bright as the blades' lit faces, so the carpet has no inside. Root ends draw as dark vertical
  stubs (the ambient-only root darkening) on top of a sunlit floor - the dark is on the blade, not under it.
Verdict: the strips-on-a-bright-floor image loses on depth. Biggest single cause: the ground under the grass
receives full sun and full sky, as if the canopy were not there.
