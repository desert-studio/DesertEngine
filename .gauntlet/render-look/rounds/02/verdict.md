# Round 02 verdict - lens: light
(Blindness nominal, BAR.md. Judged on the lens's question.)

- One image: the stalks carry light - the sun-side of each stalk is clearly brighter than its shaded side, the
  tips catch a warm sheen, and the shade inside the field is a deep, coloured shadow. Light reads as a key light.
- The other: every blade in the carpet has nearly the same value; lit side and shaded side of a blade differ
  little, there is no sheen at grazing angles, and the carpet reads as ambient-lit matte strips. The sun is
  visible on the floor and the path, barely on the grass itself.
Verdict: the uniform-value, matte-carpet image loses on light. Biggest single cause on our side: the blade
does not respond to the key light as a waxy, translucent leaf (no specular sheen, weak through-light), so the
whole carpet is carried by the sky term.
