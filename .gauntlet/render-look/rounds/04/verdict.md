# Round 04 verdict - lens: material
(Blindness nominal, BAR.md: the subject differs at a glance. Judged on the lens's question.)

- A: dry, wind-bent stalks with fine high-frequency variation; weathered clapboard reads as wood under a soft key;
  the shade inside the grass is dense and dark, and the tonal floor goes nearly black between stalks.
- B: grass blades read as grass, but the shade between them sits on a greyed floor - the deepest tones of the carpet,
  the fence posts and the barn corner never get darker than a lifted grey; the dirt path reads as saturated orange
  gravel. The reflections on the blades are not the problem: nothing glints where it should not.
Verdict: B loses on material. Biggest single cause on our side, from this pair: not the reflection term (specular
occlusion, the round's planned change, changes nothing visible - see STATE), but the floor of the tone scale -
shade reads grey, not dark, which flattens every material in it.
